**Review quality and provenance**

- **Explicit context** — `fetch --context` and `fetch-local --context` attach named context files, and findings cite them. [Explicit context](docs/manual.md#explicit-context) <!-- changesets: context-sources.md -->
- **Coverage basis** — every coverage read now names what its percentage counts: full hunk spans, both sides and context, not just changed lines. [Diff coverage](docs/manual.md#diff-coverage-agent-attestation) <!-- changesets: coverage-scoring-basis.md -->
- **Coverage attestation** — `review.json` gains an optional `coverage[]` checked against the bundle's own hunks, with `--require-coverage` to refuse a partial claim and one body line on a partial attestation. [Diff coverage](docs/manual.md#diff-coverage-agent-attestation) <!-- changesets: diff-coverage-attestation.md -->
- **`ranges: null` is malformed** — a coverage claim whose `ranges` is present but not a string now counts as unattested instead of as the whole file. [Diff coverage](docs/manual.md#diff-coverage-agent-attestation) <!-- changesets: null-ranges-malformed.md -->
- **Ignore overrides** — repeatable `--ignore <glob>` and `--no-default-excludes` on fetch, both recorded in the exclusion receipt. [Exclusions](docs/manual.md#exclusions-and-policy-silence) <!-- changesets: ignore-overrides.md -->
- **Suppression receipts** — excluded diff bytes and withheld findings are receipted, with `post --min-severity` and `report --include-withheld` to read them. [Exclusions](docs/manual.md#exclusions-and-policy-silence) <!-- changesets: ignore-suppression.md -->
- **Proposed learnings** — propose inactive one-rule packs from resolved threads, accept them through the pack importer, or reject them permanently with a reason. [Proposed learnings](docs/manual.md#proposed-learnings) <!-- changesets: learnings-propose.md -->
- **Rule import** — `learn --pack <dir> --id <rule>` imports a structured rule object and keeps its pack provenance and hash. [Import a rule object](docs/manual.md#import-a-rule-object) <!-- changesets: rule-pack-import.md -->
- **Rule exposure** — `learn --status` gains `recalled_in`, `cited_in`, `exposure`, and `acceptance` per rule, reporting unmeasured recall as null rather than zero. [Knowledge base](docs/manual.md#knowledge-base-scoped-local-rules) <!-- changesets: rule-exposure.md -->
- **Risk scoring** — an optional `risk` claim in `review.json` plus `report --risk` and `report --max-risk`, which reports the larger of your claim and a deterministic floor. [Triage risk queue](docs/manual.md#triage-risk-queue) <!-- changesets: risk-score.md -->
- **Reaction proposals** — opt-in reaction proposals from posted comments, plus per-rule acceptance and false-hit rates. [Reaction proposals](docs/manual.md#reaction-proposals-and-rule-rates) <!-- changesets: reactions-trend.md -->
- **Resolution metrics** — live resolution rollups, clean-review metrics, and calibration receipts, with clean reports getting their own headline. [Resolution and clean reviews](docs/manual.md#resolution-and-clean-reviews) <!-- changesets: resolution-rate.md -->
- **Withheld capabilities** — `post.json` records, per capability, what a host could not carry and why, so a degraded publish stays auditable. [Guarded submission](docs/manual.md#guarded-submission-and-operator-surfaces) <!-- changesets: withheld-capabilities.md -->
- **Payload receipts** — a post payload receipt now hashes the rendered review content and the reviewed head, not diff positions or range metadata. [Guarded submission](docs/manual.md#guarded-submission-and-operator-surfaces) <!-- changesets: post-content-digest.md -->
- **Partial publication** — post only the comments and summary parts missing from the fetched remote content, and rearm at a new head with `rearmed` and `previous_head` recorded. [Guarded submission](docs/manual.md#guarded-submission-and-operator-surfaces) <!-- changesets: post-content-recovery.md -->
- **Live authority** — post refreshes live reviews and comments first and refuses without writing when that read fails, so a retry no longer needs a re-fetch. [Guarded submission](docs/manual.md#guarded-submission-and-operator-surfaces) <!-- changesets: post-live-authority.md -->
- **Author fingerprint** — `fetch` writes an advisory `author.json` (`{agent, model, confidence, evidence[]}`) from five harness signals, suggests a reviewer from another model family, and `record.json` carries the author/reviewer pair that `trend` tallies. [Bundle namespace](docs/manual.md#bundle-namespace-core-files-vs-x-) <!-- changesets: author-routing.md, author-routing-hint.md, author-routing-record.md, author-routing-skill.md, author-routing-trend.md -->
- **Revert memory** — a changed path that matches the tracked reverts ledger gives `fetch` a bounded, fenced `reverts.txt`, and the review prompt treats a revert as evidence the change broke something once. [Reverts](docs/manual.md#reverts) <!-- changesets: fetch-reverts.md -->
- **Validation pass** — a trusted `prompts/validate.md` pass writes `validation.json` per sig, `rigor_policy` floors each severity tier, and `post --require-rigor` narrows it per invocation: advisory by default, a receipted withhold in gate mode. [Rigor floors](docs/manual.md#rigor-floors-and-the-validation-pass) <!-- changesets: rigor-validation.md -->
- **Published key names** — `prompts/review.md` tags the evidence rung as `rigor` and the in-bounds flag as `in-bounds`, and an archtest holds every published key to `review.schema.json`. [Rigor floors](docs/manual.md#rigor-floors-and-the-validation-pass) <!-- changesets: prompt-rigor-key.md, prompt-key-shapes.md -->
- **Verdict reconciliation** — binding verdicts reconcile separately from summary and inline content, so a retried approval does not duplicate a GitLab summary note. [Submit per host](docs/manual.md#submit-post-per-host) <!-- changesets: post-verdict-reconciliation.md -->
- **Plain-body Basis line** — `record` leaves a `review.json` body with no leading quote block byte-identical and reports no `basis-replaced` warning, because there is no confidence lead to insert under. [Review-input problems](docs/manual.md#review-input-problems) <!-- changesets: sta-425-basis-plain-body.md -->
- **Array-valued coverage ranges** — `next --coverage` refuses a `coverage[].ranges` array with the same `bad-review-input` problem `record` reports, instead of reading it as a silent 0% attestation. [Diff coverage](docs/manual.md#diff-coverage-agent-attestation) <!-- changesets: sta-425-coverage-ranges.md -->
- **Passing gate next step** — a passing `report --check <tier>` names `merge — gate passes` in the envelope's `next` and human footer, leaving `address open threads` for a failing gate. [Machine surface](docs/manual.md#machine-surface) <!-- changesets: sta-425-gate-next.md -->
- **More generated paths** — `*.gen.*` and `zz_generated.*` now count as generated in the shared role heuristic, so `next --coverage` and the risk floor exempt them like the other generated patterns. [Diff coverage](docs/manual.md#diff-coverage-agent-attestation) <!-- changesets: sta-425-generated-exemption.md -->
