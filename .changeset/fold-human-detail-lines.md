Every human output line that quotes a value now folds that value's line breaks and bidi controls to spaces, not only refusals and `»` progress lines: a `doctor` check's detail, a `calibrate` row, a `post --dry-run` diagram detail, `fix`'s finding locator and brief, `learn`'s import and rule lines, `backtest`'s mined candidates and fixture report, `loop`'s round lines, and the rows `report`, `trend`, `handoff`, `index`, `journal`, `pack`, `risk`, `gate`, `adopt`, `ingest`, and `triage` print. A path, a host's reply, or an agent's text carrying a newline could start a line that read as verdict's own `error:` or `warn:`. A block another author wrote (a finding's body and the `kb.txt` recall in `fix`, the attribution footer in a `post --dry-run` preview) keeps its lines, each led by an indented `|` tag.
